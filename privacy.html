Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Monyazone Studio operates the portfolio at monyazone.github.io. This policy also applies to games published by Monyazone Studio that link to it, including games distributed through Meta Instant Games. You can request deletion of your data by email.

Website visits and support

Our portfolio has no registration, contact forms, advertising, analytics scripts, or studio-set cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the portfolio and may process technical information, including your IP address, to deliver and protect the site. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to include. We use them to respond, investigate the issue you report, or handle your privacy request.

Game data and platform features

When you play on Meta, Meta runs the platform and handles your account under its Privacy Policy. We never ask for your Facebook password.

If a game uses a platform feature that gives the studio data, its privacy notice identifies the information the feature uses, whether it is stored, and who can see it. For example, a save feature can use a game-specific player identifier to retrieve progress, and a leaderboard can use a score and the display information needed to show that score. These features apply only to games that use them. We limit use of platform data to the disclosed game features and their support.

Any game-specific storage, analytics, advertising, or payments will be described in that game’s privacy notice before the feature is enabled, including the provider and the data involved.

Sharing and protection

Our website host and email provider process information to deliver those services. Service providers for a game are identified in its privacy notice. We may disclose information when legally required. We do not sell personal data or use Meta platform data for advertising profiles. We limit access to information we hold to people who need it to operate the relevant service or respond to a request.

Retention

We keep support correspondence while needed to handle the request and follow up. We keep any game data we hold only as long as needed for the disclosed feature. When the information is no longer needed, we delete it unless retention is required by applicable law.

Request access, correction, or deletion

  1. Email mohamedimorignaw@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request.
  2. Tell us the name of the game or website and what you would like deleted. If the game shows a player or support ID, include it so we can find your record. Keep passwords and payment details out of your message.
  3. We will confirm receipt and may ask for the minimum information needed to verify the request. We will promptly delete the data we control unless legally required to retain it, and tell you when the request has been resolved or if we found no data to delete.

You can use the same email address to request access or correction. To manage information held by Meta, use your Facebook privacy and account settings.

Children and changes to this policy

Players must meet the eligibility requirements of the platform and any age requirements shown for the game. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child shared personal information with us, contact us using the address above so we can review it and remove it where appropriate.

When we update this policy, we will post the revised text and update the date at the top of this page.
